A Witness to Your Becoming

I have seen you struggle, I have seen you endure, and I hope I get to see you become everything you've dreamed of.

homequietlovelife

I have witnessed the moments when you struggled, the moments when you were exhausted, and the moments when you wanted to give up but couldn't because you still had dreams you wanted to fulfill—not for anyone else, but for yourself.

I've seen how you tried to carry things that sometimes felt far too heavy for one person. I've seen how you pushed through an entire day even when you were already exhausted, how you chose to get back up even after losing the motivation to do so, and how you convinced yourself that you could keep going even during the moments when you simply wanted to stop.

And I know that none of it has been easy for you.

There were days when it felt like the world simply refused to cooperate. There were nights filled with exhaustion and mornings when you had to get up even though all you wanted was to stay in bed. There were moments when you questioned whether you were still on the right path, whether you could keep going, and whether all the exhaustion you were giving for that dream would eventually be worth it.

But despite everything, you kept going.

And that's one of the things I admire most about you.

Not because you're always strong, but because even when you know you can get tired, you still learn to move forward. Not because you never get hurt, but because even when you do, you still choose to get back up. Not because you don't have fears, but because you don't let those fears make your decisions for you.

And I hope that when the day comes when you forget how far you've already come, you'll remember that there was someone who witnessed every step you took.

I saw the nights you stayed up late studying. I saw the days when you barely had any energy left but still had things you needed to do. I heard you rant about the things that exhausted you, and I also heard the happiness in your voice whenever there was something, no matter how small, that you were proud of.

And through every one of those moments, I admired you even more.

I don't wish for you to never feel tired again. I don't wish for you to never feel sad or discouraged either. I know those things are part of life, and I don't want to promise you a life without hardship because I know the world doesn't work that way.

All I want is for you to remember that when the days come when you feel like you can't do it anymore, you don't have to believe that you have to face everything alone.

If you need to rest, then rest.

If you need to cry, then cry.

If you need to talk about everything you've been keeping inside, tell me.

And if you have nothing left to say, we can simply sit in silence.

I won't rush you.

I won't force you to be strong right away.

I'll simply stay here while you slowly regain the strength that life has taken from you.

Because I don't want to love only the version of you who is happy, put together, and full of energy. I want to know you on the days when you're exhausted, when your thoughts are a mess, when you're full of doubts, and when you don't know how you're going to face the next day.

I want to be a witness not only to the days when you win, but also to the days when you have to fight quietly.

And when the day finally comes when you achieve the dreams you've been fighting so hard for, I want you to remember the version of yourself who almost gave up but chose to keep going.

Because you were the one who found a way to get there.

You were the one who stayed up late.

You were the one who endured.

You were the one who fought.

And you are also the one who deserves to experience all the beautiful things you're fighting for now.

So while you're walking toward your dreams, I hope you never forget to be kind to yourself.

You don't always have to be fast.

You don't always have to be excellent.

You don't always have to prove to everyone that you can do it.

Sometimes, doing the best you can on that particular day is enough.

And if the day comes when you feel like giving up again, I hope you remember that being tired doesn't mean you have to stop.

You can pause for a while.

You can breathe.

You can rest.

And when you're ready, you can continue again.

And if you ever need someone to believe in you while you're struggling to believe in yourself, I'm here.

Not to carry the entire world for you, because I know you are capable of facing your own battles.

I'm here to remind you that you don't have to face every battle alone.

I have witnessed how much you've struggled.

I've witnessed how exhausted you've become.

And someday, I want to be there to witness the day you finally say, "I did it."

And when that day comes, I hope you'll smile and look back at everything you've been through.

Because every night you thought you wouldn't make it, every tear you wiped away on your own, every exhaustion you endured, and every moment you chose to get back up when you wanted to give up—all of it became part of the journey toward the person you've always dreamed of becoming.

And when you finally reach the dream you've been chasing for so long, I hope you remember to congratulate yourself.

Because you've been fighting for a long time.

And through all those moments, even when you didn't notice, there was someone quietly admiring you.

That someone is me.

And I will always support you—not because you have to be perfect, but simply because you are you.

And that will always be enough for me.

Written in the quiet hours, mostly for me—and now, for you too.
Cathlynn.
