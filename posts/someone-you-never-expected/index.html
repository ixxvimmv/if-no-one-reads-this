Someone You Never Expected

Yet somehow, I became the answer to a prayer you never knew you made.

lettershomelove

I didn't know this is what it feels like to become someone's answered prayer.

You’ve told me so many times that I’ve become an answered prayer for you, and every time you say it, I still don't know exactly how to respond. I can't fully explain this roller-coaster of emotions that comes over me whenever I hear those words from you. There’s happiness, disbelief, warmth, and a kind of gratitude that I don't even know how to put into words.

But yes, I love hearing it from you.

I love hearing you say that I've become an answered prayer for you. I don't think I'll ever get tired of hearing it, no matter how many times you tell me. You could tell me tomorrow, the next day, and a hundred more times after that, and I'd probably still smile the same way.

Because there's something so beautiful about knowing that my presence in your life means that much to you.

I've always thought of answered prayers as something grand—something you've waited years for, something you've asked the universe for in the quietest moments, something that arrives when you least expect it. I never imagined that an answered prayer could be a person. Someone you meet on an ordinary night. Someone you slowly get attached to through conversations, laughter, late-night calls, random stories, and all the little moments that didn't seem significant at first.

And somehow, that person became me.

I don't know what I did to deserve being called someone's answered prayer. I don't know if I can ever fully understand what you saw in me that made you feel that way. But I know one thing: I will never take those words lightly.

Because if I am an answered prayer to you, then you should know that you became something I never knew I was praying for, too.

Maybe I just didn't know how to name it before you came along.

I didn't know I was looking for someone whose voice would become my comfort. I didn't know I needed someone I could talk to until midnight about everything and nothing. I didn't know I would care about someone's day so much that I'd want to hear every little detail, even the parts you think aren't worth telling. I didn't know I'd find myself remembering your favorite color, your little habits, your random stories, and all the tiny things that make you who you are.

I didn't know I was waiting for you.

And maybe that's why hearing you call me your answered prayer means so much.

Because while you tell me that I am something you prayed for, I'm slowly realizing that you have become something I would be afraid to lose.

So no, I don't care how many times you tell me.

Tell me again.

Tell me when you're happy.

Tell me when we're laughing.

Tell me during one of our random late-night conversations.

Tell me when you're tired and we barely have anything to talk about.

Tell me even when you think I've already heard it too many times.

I will still listen.

I will still smile.

And somewhere inside me, I'll still think about how incredibly lucky I am that someone like you looks at me and sees an answered prayer.

Maybe I'll never find the perfect words to explain what that feels like.

But perhaps I don't need to.

Because whenever you tell me, I think my heart already knows how to answer.

I love that.

I love knowing that I became something good in your life.

I love knowing that my existence could bring you comfort.

I love knowing that somewhere between an ordinary midnight and all the conversations that followed, I became someone you once prayed for.

And more than anything—

I love you.

Maybe that's the simplest answer I can give.

You call me your answered prayer, but I hope you know that loving you feels like an answered prayer I never knew I had.
Written in the quiet hours, mostly for me—and now, for you too.
Cathlynn.
