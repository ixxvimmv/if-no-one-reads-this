Can I Call You When I Get Home?

I used to hate phone calls, but now, they're my favorite.

lettershomelove
I hate phone calls, but you're the exception. 

Before I met you, I never understood how people could spend hours talking on the phone. I always thought they were exhausting. I'd rather send a message than answer a call. It felt easier that way—quieter, simpler, and less overwhelming. I never imagined that one person could completely change the way I looked at something I used to avoid.

Then I met you.

Whether you call me at night after you've gotten home or while you're busy with something—studying; reviewing for a demo, quiz, or exam; organizing your notes; or even doing your makeup—I always look forward to hearing from you. Somehow, without me even realizing it, phone calls with you have become my favorite part of the day.

I love hearing your voice. I love it when you randomly start singing, even if it's only for a few seconds. I love listening to you tell me stories about your day—about your duties, the little things that made you smile, the moments that exhausted you, or the random thoughts that crossed your mind. Somehow, even the stories you think are ordinary become my favorite ones simply because they're yours. And no matter how your day goes, you always ask me about mine, even though my answer is almost always, "Nothing much happened."

But the truth is, something always does happen. I get to hear your voice, and somehow, that's enough to make an ordinary day feel a little less ordinary.

It's funny because I've always preferred silence. I enjoy being alone, and I've always believed silence was enough for me. But with you, silence feels different. It isn't empty, and it certainly isn't awkward. It's peaceful.

Sometimes, we don't even have anything important to talk about. Sometimes, we're simply there, keeping each other company without saying much at all. You study while I quietly do my own thing. I scroll through my phone while you review your notes. Sometimes you hum a song under your breath without realizing it. Sometimes we tease each other over the smallest things until one of us starts laughing. And sometimes, we sit in complete silence, yet somehow, it never feels uncomfortable.

I've learned that silence doesn't always mean there's nothing left to say. Sometimes, it simply means you've found someone you're comfortable enough to say nothing with. And somehow, I found that person in you.

Then there are nights when neither of us notices how late it's become. What feels like a few minutes somehow turns into hours. We tell ourselves we'll end the call after one more story, one more joke, or one more question, but "one more" never really means one more.

Sometimes, we even fall asleep while the call is still going. Those moments might seem ordinary to everyone else, but they're among my favorites. Waking up in the middle of the night, hearing your soft breathing on the other end of the line, and realizing neither of us remembered to hang up somehow makes me smile before I drift back to sleep. It's such a simple thing, yet it brings me a kind of comfort I never knew I needed.

I never thought a phone call could feel like home, but yours somehow does.

I think that's my favorite part about us. You never have to try too hard. You don't have to say anything extraordinary, and you don't have to entertain me every second we're on the phone. Your presence has always been enough, and maybe that's what love looks like. Not always in grand confessions or dramatic gestures, but in hearing someone's sleepy voice before they drift off, listening to them breathe while the world grows quiet around you, or staying on the phone even when neither of you has anything left to say because simply being there is enough.

Maybe that's why I never get tired of your calls. Every time my phone lights up with your name, I still smile before answering because I already know that, for the next few hours, the world will become a little quieter, a little lighter, and a little easier to carry.

You have no idea how much comfort your voice brings me. You have no idea how many ordinary days you've unknowingly made beautiful just by calling. You have no idea how someone who once hated phone calls now secretly hopes you'll call again before the day ends.

And maybe that's the funniest part of all.

I thought I hated phone calls. Turns out, I was just waiting for the right person to be on the other end of the line.

Because no matter what we're talking about—or even if we're talking about nothing at all—as long as it's you, I'll always answer. And if one day we accidentally fall asleep before saying goodnight again, I hope we never apologize for it. Because somehow, even in sleep, knowing you're still there makes the distance between us feel a little smaller.

Maybe that's why your voice has become my favorite sound. Not because it's perfect, but because somehow, it always feels like home.
Written in the quiet hours, mostly for me—and now, for you too.
Cathlynn.
