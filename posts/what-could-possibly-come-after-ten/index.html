What Could Possibly Come After Ten?

Maybe there was never supposed to be a “but” when it comes to you.

lettershomelove

Do you know the “10/10” trend? The “She's a 10, but…” one? For sure, you've probably thought about it yourself at some point—like, “I'm a 10, but I can't cook a proper meal.” Hahahaha, just kidding. I honestly don't care whether you can cook or not. You could burn the kitchen trying to make eggs, and I'd probably still give you a 10.

Because for me, you're a complete 10 out of 10.

No but.

You're a 10 because you take care of other people. You make sure everyone is okay, and somehow, you even find a way to take care of me despite having your own battles to fight. You never fail to check up on me, and you do the same for the people around you. You don't do it simply because you're becoming a nurse. You do it because caring comes naturally to you. It's one of the ways you show your love—quietly, sincerely, and without expecting anything in return. You notice when someone isn't okay. You remember the little things. You ask if someone has eaten, if they've arrived safely, and if they're doing fine. You have this way of making people feel cared for without even realizing how much those little gestures mean. And I hope you know that I notice them, too.

You're a 10 because you still show up even when you're busy and tired, as if you're not carrying battles of your own. You have your own deadlines, responsibilities, exhaustion, and moments when you probably just want to lie down and forget about everything for a while, yet somehow, you still make time for the people you love. You still reply, you still call, you still ask how my day went, and you still listen to my random stories. Even when you're exhausted, you somehow make me feel like I deserve a little piece of your time. I don't take that for granted. I know your time is precious, especially when you already have so much on your plate, and the fact that you choose to share even a little of it with me means more than you probably realize.

You're a 10 because you have such a soft heart, and I know I should take care of it as if it were something precious—because it is. I know the world can be harsh, and I know you've experienced moments that could have made you colder, yet somehow, you still choose to care. You still choose to be gentle. You still choose to love people despite knowing that loving someone can sometimes hurt. And that softness of yours is something I never want to take advantage of. I want to protect it, not change it. I want to be someone who makes you feel safe enough to keep being soft, even when the world gives you reasons not to be.

You're a 10 because you make people feel seen. You have this way of making someone feel like their presence matters, whether they're telling you something important or simply rambling about the most random thing. You listen, you remember, and you respond in a way that makes people feel like they're worth your attention. I know there are probably moments when you don't realize you're doing it, but I've noticed how naturally you make people feel heard. And maybe that's one of the things I admire most about you—you don't need grand gestures to make someone feel important. Sometimes, simply being there is already enough.

You're a 10 because you let yourself be yourself around me. You don't always have to be composed, serious, or the strong person everyone expects you to be. You can be loud, quiet, silly, dramatic, sleepy, clingy, or completely random. You can yap about something for an hour without realizing how much time has passed, randomly sing during our calls, complain about your day, tell me a story that has absolutely no ending, or simply stay quiet while we do our own things. And I love that I get to see those sides of you. I don't want only the polished version of you. I want the real you—the one who doesn't have to pretend around me.

You're a 10 because you're human. You get tired. You make mistakes. You overthink. You have bad days. Sometimes you don't know what you're doing, and sometimes you question whether you're doing enough. You don't always have everything figured out, and you don't have to. I don't love you because I think you're perfect. I love you because you're you, and somehow, even the imperfect parts of you have become things I wouldn't want to erase. Your flaws don't make you less deserving of love. They simply make you human, and I want to love you as a whole person, not just the parts that are easy to love.

You're a 10 because you keep trying. Even when things don't go the way you planned, you still find a way to move forward. Even when you're exhausted, you still get up and continue. Even when you doubt yourself, there's still a part of you that refuses to give up on the life you're trying to build. I know there are days when everything feels overwhelming, but I hope you know that I see your effort. I see how hard you're trying, even when you think nobody notices. And I'll always be proud of you for choosing to continue, especially on the days when continuing is the hardest thing to do.

You're a 10 because of the girl you used to be, the woman you are now, and the nurse you're slowly becoming. I know how much you've worked for that dream, and I know becoming the person you want to be isn't always easy. There will be exhausting duties, sleepless nights, difficult examinations, return demonstrations, mistakes, and moments when you'll wonder whether you're really capable of becoming the nurse you dreamed of being. But I hope you never forget how far you've already come. I see the little girl who once had a dream, the girl who kept going despite everything, and the woman who is now slowly turning that dream into reality. And when the day finally comes when someone calls you “Nurse,” I'll be one of the people quietly smiling and thinking about every difficult day that brought you there.

You're a 10 because you make ordinary things feel special. A random phone call, a late-night conversation, a simple “have you eaten?”, a message telling me about your day, a picture of something pink because you know it's your favorite color, a random song, or even just a few minutes of your time—somehow, you've made these little things mean so much to me. You don't have to do anything extraordinary to make me happy. Sometimes, your presence alone is enough. That's what I love about us. We can be doing absolutely nothing, and somehow, I still feel like I'm exactly where I want to be.

You're a 10 because you've become my comfort person. You're the person I look for when I have something I want to share, the person I want to hear from at the end of the day, and the person whose name can instantly make me smile. Somewhere along the way, talking to you became something I look forward to, and hearing your voice became one of my favorite parts of the day. I never thought I'd be someone who enjoyed phone calls this much, but then again, maybe phone calls were never the problem. Maybe I just needed the right person on the other end of the line.

You're a 10 because you make me want to become softer, too. Loving you has taught me that love doesn't always have to be loud or grand. Sometimes, it is simply listening to your stories, remembering the things you casually mention, checking if you've eaten, reminding you to rest, waiting until you get home safely, and being there when you need someone. You make me want to love intentionally—to make sure that the person I love never has to question whether she is loved. And if I can give you even a fraction of the comfort and happiness you've given me, then I'll consider myself lucky.

You're a 10 because you're someone I can admire without putting you on a pedestal. I don't need to pretend you're perfect to know how special you are. I can see your struggles, your tired days, your insecurities, your mistakes, and the parts of yourself you might not always like, and somehow, none of those things make me love you less. If anything, they make me want to understand you more. Because loving someone isn't about loving only their brightest days. It's about staying gentle with them when the light isn't as bright.

And maybe that's why, if someone asks me, “She's a 10, but…”, I'd probably interrupt them before they could even finish.

Because what but?

What could I possibly add after 10?

You're a 10 when you're happy, a 10 when you're tired, a 10 when you're dressed up, and a 10 when you're still doing your makeup. You're a 10 when you're studying, when you're complaining about your duties, when you're sleepy, when you're being dramatic, when you're yapping, and even when you're quiet. You're a 10 when you feel confident, and you're still a 10 when you're struggling to see anything good about yourself.

Your worth doesn't decrease just because you have a bad day.

So maybe the trend asks, “She's a 10, but…”

But I'd rather change the question.

She's a 10.

And that's it.

No but.

No exceptions.

No conditions.

No need to prove anything.

Just you.

And if I had to rate how much I love you?

10 out of 10 wouldn't even come close.

And maybe that's why I could never put a limit on how much I can love you. Every day, you give me another reason to admire you, another little thing to appreciate, and another side of you that makes me think, how did I get so lucky to know someone like you? I don't think I'll ever run out of reasons to love you, because the more I know you, the more I see how much there is to love.

You're not a 10 because you're perfect. You're a 10 because even in the moments when you don't feel like one, I'll still see everything that makes you worth loving.

So if the question is, “She's a 10, but…”—I don't think I could ever finish that sentence.

Because there is no but.

There is just you.

And somehow, that's more than enough for me.
