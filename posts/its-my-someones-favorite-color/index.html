It's My Someone's Favorite Color

If I can't carry you everywhere, I'll carry little pieces of you instead.

homememorylove

Many people thought my favorite color was pink. They thought it was my branding, my signature color—that's why some of them would gift me something pink. They'd see anything pink and immediately think of me. But no one knows, not even my parents, that my actual favorite color is blue.

The reason I have so many pink things, whether they're inside or outside my bag—keychains, phone cases, little accessories, and even the smallest things I carry with me—is that pink is someone's favorite color.

Someone I can call mine.

Someone who became my comfort person.

Someone whose own little branding is the color pink.

The moment I found out that pink was your favorite color, I don't think you realized how much that tiny piece of information stayed with me. It was such a simple thing to know about you, but somehow, it became something I started carrying with me everywhere. I began putting little pieces of pink into my own things—not because pink suddenly became my favorite color, but because I wanted to have something that reminded me of you.

Maybe it sounds silly.

It's just a color.

But sometimes, the smallest things become meaningful when they're connected to someone you love.

Every pink keychain I put on my bag, every pink phone case I chose, every little pink thing I decided to keep became a quiet way of keeping you close. It felt like carrying a tiny piece of you wherever I went. I didn't need a photograph or a reminder written somewhere. Sometimes, all I needed was to see that familiar color, and suddenly, you were the first person that came to mind.

And whenever I see something pink, my first instinct is still to message you.

"Look, it's your favorite color."

Or sometimes, I don't even have anything else to say.

"It's pink."

As if you don't already know.

As if I don't already know.

But somehow, I still want to tell you.

Because I love sharing little things with you. Even the most ordinary moments feel a little more special when I can turn them into something that reminds me of you. I could be walking somewhere and suddenly notice a pink flower, a pink sign, a pink bag, or some random pink object sitting on a shelf, and before I even realize it, I've already thought of you.

It's funny how a color can become a person.

How something that used to be nothing more than a shade can suddenly carry someone's laugh, their voice, their stories, and their presence.

Pink became you to me.

And maybe that's why I never correct people when they assume it's my favorite color. I just smile and let them believe it. I don't really mind if they think pink is part of who I am.

Because in a way, they're not entirely wrong.

Pink did become a part of me.

Just not because I chose it for myself.

I chose it because it reminds me of you.

Blue may be the color I've always loved, but pink became the color I learned to love because of someone else. And there's something strangely beautiful about that—the fact that loving someone can make you find meaning in something you never thought would mean anything to you.

So maybe pink isn't my favorite color.

Maybe it's just your color that I get to carry with me.

And whenever I see it, I'll probably still think of you.

I'll still want to send you a picture.

I'll still want to say, "Look, it's your favorite color."

Because somewhere along the way, I stopped seeing pink as just pink.

I started seeing you.

Written in the quiet hours, mostly for me—and now, for you too.
Cathlynn.
