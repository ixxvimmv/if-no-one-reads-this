To the Nursing Student I Met at Midnight

A love story written between late-night conversations and unspoken feelings.

lettershomelove

On a random midnight, I never realized that I would find you. 

It was just another ordinary night. We talked. We shared our own stories. We laughed. And somehow, everything felt so unreal. It's funny how I ended our conversation that night because I thought you might find me annoying with all the things I had said. But then, after a couple of days, we talked again—and this time, we clicked. 

We started talking every day and every night. We stayed up late simply because we enjoyed each other's company. We talked again. We laughed again. We shared our stories again—whether they were filled with happiness or weighed down by sadness. We didn't just exchange stories; we slowly tied our souls together until they felt like one. And I admit that was the first time I had ever experienced something like that—meeting someone who understood my soul and saw parts of themselves in it. I loved that feeling. It felt like I had finally found a place where I didn't have to explain why I am the way I am.

But I know, and I'm aware that's not how it works. No one is made to understand another person completely. We're all different. We carry different fears, different scars, different dreams, and different ways of loving. And maybe that's what makes it so beautiful.

Because despite all those differences, you still chose to stay.

You stayed through my random thoughts at two in the morning. You listened to stories that had no ending. You laughed at jokes that weren't even funny. You made room for every version of me—the loud one, the quiet one, and the one who simply wanted to sit in comfortable silence.

Without realizing it, you became part of my routine.

My day somehow felt incomplete if I didn't get to tell you about the smallest things that happened. Every notification made me hope it was you. Every midnight became something I looked forward to because I knew there was a chance I'd get to spend it with you. Somehow, the hours never felt enough. No matter how long we talked, I'd always find another story to tell, another question to ask, another reason to stay awake a little longer if it meant spending more time with you.

I would have never imagined that because of that one random night, I would have a nurse by my side.

It's funny how life works. Out of all the people I could have met that midnight, it led me to you. Someone whose hands are meant to heal, yet somehow ended up healing parts of me you never even knew were hurting. I never expected that a random conversation would become my favorite part of every day or that the person I once thought I might annoy would become the person I couldn't wait to talk to.

It's strange how love doesn't always arrive with grand confessions or dramatic moments. Sometimes, it quietly grows from calling each other "Baby" as a joke until it no longer feels like one. Sometimes, it hides in shared laughter, in comfortable silences, and in remembering the little things about each other. Sometimes, it begins on an ordinary midnight that was never supposed to be memorable.

And maybe that's how I fell for you.

Not all at once, but little by little.

Conversation after conversation.

Midnight, after midnight.

Until one day, I caught myself smiling at my phone before I even opened your message. Until your name became my favorite notification. Until your presence started feeling less like coincidence and more like something I silently thanked the universe for.

Sometimes I catch myself smiling just thinking about how everything started. If I had gone to sleep a little earlier, if you had never sent that message, or if one of us had decided not to interact, maybe our paths would have never crossed. But

They did.

And now, every midnight reminds me of the night I found you—the nurse who unknowingly became the calm in my chaos, the comfort in my restless thoughts, and the person who made me believe that the best things in life don't always come with a plan.

I don't know what tomorrow has planned for us. I don't know where these late-night conversations will eventually lead. But if I could choose one place to be right now, it would still be here—with you, talking about everything and nothing, letting another midnight pass without ever noticing the time.

Because somehow, every midnight has become my favorite part of the day.

It's where I found you.

And if I'm lucky enough, maybe it's where I'll continue falling in love with you, over and over again.

Written in the quiet hours, mostly for me—and now, for you too.
Cathlynn.
