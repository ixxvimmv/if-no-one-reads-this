Women Who Have Words

loveattentionprosecreative writing
It's the way she talks that ruins me before she even lays a hand on me; she doesn’t just fuck—she narrates it.

She doesn't whisper sweet nothings; she spits poetry laced with filth, intellect curled into sin. Every word she says is a stroke, a thrust, a command I'm too eager to obey.

She presses me back with nothing but a quote, slides her tongue between my thoughts, and then her fingers follow. and when she finally climbs on top of me, she reads me like a page—lips on my neck, nails in my chest, reciting dirty sonnets with every grind of her hips. Her vocabulary is laced with lust, and she knows exactly how to weaponize it.


She fucks me like she’s writing a masterpiece—deliberate, wild, no line wasted. Every moan she pulls from me is punctuation. Every gasp, a paragraph break. And just when I think I'm finished, she starts again—with words, with teeth, with the kind of rhythm that makes me forget how to speak.


Because with her, I don't just get touched—I get written. And I'll let her write all over me, fuck me full of metaphors, and leave her verses dripping between my legs.


Yes, I love to be fucked by women who have words, and they always leave me speechless.
Written in the quiet hours, mostly for me—and now, for you too.
Cathlynn.
