An Engineer's Guide for the Physics Love

journalcreativityprosecreative writing

When you're in love—and you see the world through the lens of engineering—everything begins to feel like a system. Love isn't just emotion. It's applied physics, a set of interwoven laws, reactions, and forces that shape your inner universe with measurable precision.


STATICS

You analyze the forces acting on your heart, trying to remain balanced. you brace yourself, calculating the support reactions: how much of this feeling can your emotional frame handle before you yield? you model the moment they walk into a room and become the external load that changes everything. equilibrium? impossible. you're already shifting.


DYNAMICS

your heart is in motion. you feel acceleration, not just of your pulse, but of your thoughts, your decisions. newton's second law is now emotional law: your reaction to them is proportional to the force they exert on your world. The moment becomes kinetic, not potential anymore. you're in freefall, but it's not chaos—it's governed motion.


KINEMATICS

kinematics becomes the poetry of motion. the arc of their smile, the curve of a shared glance—it's a trajectory with meaning. displacement becomes more than spatial; it's emotional. you track the path of your growing affection like plotting motion under constant acceleration.


WORK AND ENERGY

work and energy describe the effort it takes to keep the connection strong. Every word spoken, every time spent—it's joules of effort toward an emotional bond. You're a machine with no fatigue limit, and yet you learn that conservation of energy applies here too. give too much, and you're depleted; love, like a good system, needs efficiency.


THERMODYNAMICS

thermodynamics sets in as things heat up. you exchange warmth—literal and figurative. ¹ first law: your love doesn't appear from nowhere; it's energy transformed from experiences, memories, intentions. ² second law: sometimes, you feel the entropy creeping in—the uncertainty, the distance, the misunderstandings. you engineer your way through it, insulating the bond, keeping the system closed but not isolated.


FLUID MECHANICS

love flows. communication becomes a fluid, its viscosity determined by trust. too many barriers, and you get turbulent relationships; but with alignment and clarity, it’s smooth, laminar flow. pressure builds in silence, but venting gently avoids cavitation of the heart. bernoulli’s principle reminds you: where love flows fastest, pressure may be lowest—don't take speed for strength.


ELECTROMAGNETISM 

electromagnetism pulses through every touch. the field you emit around each other is palpable— an invisible influence like a charged body drawing another close. capacitance builds when you store all the things you want to say. resistance? it's what you feel when you're not sure they feel the same. but when the circuit is closed— when you connect— it's pure current, energy flowing freely, lighting up your world.


WAVES AND OPTICS 

through waves and optics, you see and are seen. tou learn how to transmit without distortion, to resonate at the right frequency. you watch the interference patterns of your lives overlapping. reflection and refraction happen, but clarity returns when you align your lenses. love, like light, travels both as wave and particle— sometimes ethereal, sometimes solid.


MODERN PHYSICS 

you abandon certainty and embrace the quantum nature of love. you're both wavefunctions— only collapsing into certainty when observed. entanglement becomes more than a phenomenon; it becomes the explanation for how you're still affected by them across distance, time, and space.


MATERIALS SCIENCE 

materials science teaches you how you and they are made. you test each other's stress-strain behavior, learn ductility and resilience. some days, you're elastic, bouncing back from conflict; other days, you're plastic, permanently changed. the fracture point is real, but engineers design for safety— and so you learn how to reinforce each other, build stronger alloys of emotion and understanding.


CONTROL SYSTEMS

control systems come into play as your relationship grows. you identify feedback loops— positive ones that reinforce joy, and negative ones that stabilize tension. you tune your emotions like PID controllers: proportional in reaction, integrative in memory, derivative in prediction. stability is engineered, not assumed. and lastly;


ENGINEERING PHYSICS

reminds you that love, like any great design, is not static. it evolves with conditions, with external forces, with wear and time. but with the right knowledge— of forces, of flows, of energy and waves— you don't just fall in love. you build it. sustain it. optimize it. because to the engineer in love, the universe isn't just governed by physics— it's written in it.


Written in the quiet hours, mostly for me—and now, for you too.
Cathlynn.
