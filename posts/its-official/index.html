It's Official

lettershomelove
It's been five months since we met and almost two months since I became your suitor. And with that small span of time—from days to weeks, until it eventually turned into months—it's still such a short amount of time, but somehow, it feels like we've been together for years. It's fascinating how someone can become such a familiar part of your life in such a little time, how conversations slowly turn into routines, how the smallest updates become something you look forward to every day, and how an ordinary moment can suddenly feel so much more meaningful simply because you get to share it with that one person. I never really understood how people could feel so comfortable with someone in such a short amount of time, but then you came along, and I found myself experiencing exactly that. It's as if we've known each other long before our paths crossed, as if there's a certain familiarity between us that makes everything feel so natural, and as if loving you has become one of the easiest things my heart has ever learned to do.

I guess that's what happens when you find someone who feels like home, someone whose presence you eventually become accustomed to, not because you get tired of having them around, but because having them in your everyday life becomes something you genuinely cherish. You don't really count the days anymore; instead, you find yourself looking forward to all the little things that come with having them around, whether it's hearing about how their day went, listening to their random stories, sharing the most insignificant details, or simply knowing that somewhere in the middle of everything happening in your separate lives, you still have each other to come home to. And somewhere along the way, without either of us noticing just how much things were changing, we started building something that neither of us wanted to let go of.

And yes—you said yes to me. Exactly at 12:18 AM on November 3, you said, "We're official."

I didn't get you at first, but then you explained and explained until I slowly understood what you were trying to tell me, and it's been an hour since it finally sank in on me. I was genuinely flabbergasted because, what do you mean we were just crying before you said yes to me? What do you mean I had been feeling so much pressure just a while ago, opening up to you and telling you everything that had been weighing on my mind? What do you mean we were both feeling so down, trying to figure things out and comfort one another, and then, in just a snap, we suddenly turned into this lively, lovey-dovey couple who couldn't stop expressing how happy we were? I swear, if someone had told me that this was how our night would end, I probably would've laughed because I couldn't have imagined that a night that started with so many emotions would eventually become one of the nights I'd want to remember for the rest of my life.

How does a night go from holding back tears to holding onto something we've both been waiting for? How can two people who were just trying to make sense of their feelings suddenly find themselves celebrating a new chapter of their relationship? It's almost amusing when I think about it, but at the same time, there's something so beautiful about how everything unfolded. We didn't have to plan for the perfect moment, prepare ourselves for some grand confession, or wait until everything around us was completely settled before we could finally have this conversation. It just happened, in the middle of an ordinary night, after an honest conversation, through all the explanations, reassurances, and emotions that came with it. And perhaps that's what makes it even more special to me, because it wasn't some perfectly orchestrated moment; it was simply us, being ourselves, figuring things out together, and somehow finding our way into something we've both been looking forward to.

But perhaps that's one of the things I love most about us: we don't always have to be at our happiest to find something beautiful in each other, and we don't have to pretend that everything is okay just to keep things light between us. We can have difficult conversations, cry when things become overwhelming, admit when we're scared or uncertain, and still find comfort in knowing that neither of us has to face those feelings alone. I want you to know that you don't have to be the happiest version of yourself whenever you're with me, because I want to be there for the days when you're feeling a little less like yourself, too. I want to understand the things you sometimes struggle to explain, listen to the stories you keep repeating, reassure you when your thoughts become too loud, and remind you that you are loved even on the days when you find it difficult to feel that way. I don't want our relationship to become a place where we only celebrate the good things; I want it to be a place where we can also be honest about the difficult ones.

I know that becoming official doesn't automatically mean that everything will suddenly be perfect, that we'll never misunderstand each other, or that we'll always know exactly what to say whenever one of us is having a difficult time. There will still be days when we're tired, days when our schedules don't align, days when we have different perspectives, and days when life becomes a little too overwhelming for either of us, but I hope that through all of those things, we never lose the willingness to understand one another, communicate even when it's uncomfortable, apologize when we've made mistakes, and choose to be gentle with each other even when things aren't as easy as we'd like them to be. I don't expect us to have everything figured out right away, and I don't expect either of us to be perfect partners, because we're both still learning, growing, and discovering what it means to love someone in ways that make them feel understood and appreciated.

I don't want us to rush through this just because we finally have a label to call what we've been building for the past few months, because I still want to experience everything with you at our own pace, without feeling like we have to accomplish every little milestone immediately just because we're finally here. I want the ordinary days, the random conversations that somehow last for hours, the little dates that we'll probably talk about even after they happen, the laughter over things that nobody else would understand, the quiet moments when we don't necessarily have to say anything, and even the difficult conversations that teach us how to love each other better. I want to keep learning about you, discovering the little things you haven't told me yet, understanding the different sides of you that I haven't had the chance to meet, and falling in love with every version of you that I get to encounter as we grow older and experience more of life together. I want to be someone you can celebrate your achievements with, someone you can lean on when things become difficult, and someone who will always remind you that you don't have to figure everything out on your own.

And now, I get to call you my girlfriend. My love, my favorite person, my someone. I get to introduce you as the woman I love, and I get to look forward to all the little things that come with being able to call this relationship ours. God, it still feels so unreal typing those words, especially when I think about how everything started, how we went from getting to know each other to becoming each other's constant, and how the person I once hoped would say yes to me is now someone I can proudly call my girlfriend. If only I could go back to the beginning and tell the version of myself who was still gathering the courage to pursue you that one day, after all the conversations, the uncertainties, the reassurances, and the little moments we would eventually collect, I would get to call you mine, I think that version of me would have the biggest smile on their face.

Five months ago, we were just two people who happened to cross paths, almost two months ago, I gathered the courage to pursue you and show you how much I wanted to get to know you beyond what we already shared, and today, November 3, we get to look back at all those little moments and realize that they somehow led us here. It's funny how time works, because although it hasn't been that long since we first met, I already have so many memories with you that I want to keep close to my heart, and now that we're officially together, I can't help but feel excited about all the memories we haven't made yet, all the places we haven't visited, all the conversations we haven't had, and all the little milestones that are still waiting for us somewhere in the future.

I hope you know how happy I am that it's you and how grateful I am that, out of all the possibilities life could have given us, somehow, we found each other at the right time and decided to give this a chance. I know there are still so many things ahead of us, and I know we'll encounter moments that will challenge our patience, understanding, and commitment, but I hope we continue to find reasons to stay, reasons to communicate, and reasons to fall in love with each other all over again. I hope that years from now, when we look back at November 3, we'll remember how an ordinary night, filled with tears and conversations, unexpectedly became the night we officially started calling each other girlfriends, and we'll laugh about how I couldn't even understand what was happening at first because I was too overwhelmed to process that you had finally said yes.

So, here's to us, my love. Here's to the beginning of something we've already been taking care of for months, to the little things that brought us closer, to the love that continues to grow between us, and to all the versions of ourselves we'll get to meet as we navigate this relationship together. Here's to more firsts, more memories, more dates, more late-night conversations, more laughter, more reassurance, and more ordinary days that will eventually become some of our most treasured memories. I may not know exactly what the future has prepared for us, but I know that I want to keep experiencing it with you, one day at a time, without rushing through the beautiful things we're still about to discover.

It's official. You're my girlfriend, and I'm yours. And if there's one thing I want you to remember from all of this, it's that I didn't just want to become your girlfriend; I wanted to become someone you could continue choosing, trusting, growing with, and loving through every season of life. Thank you for saying yes to me, for allowing me to love you in this way, and for giving us the chance to turn everything we've been building into something we can finally call ours.

I love you so much, Asaiah Ysabel. Here's to us, and hopefully, to a lifetime of being each other's favorite person.
