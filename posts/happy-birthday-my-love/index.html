Happy Birthday, My Love!!

lettershomelove

Happy birthday to the plot twist that i never expected—to the woman i love.

If someone told me before that one day, someone would come into my life so unexpectedly and somehow become one of the most important parts of it, I probably wouldn't have believed them. I never planned for you, never knew I was looking for someone like you, and yet somehow, life brought you to me at the most unexpected time.

And now, here we are.

Another year of your life, and i feel incredibly lucky that i get to witness it. I get to know the little things about you, listen to your stories, hear your voice, see your smile, and be there through the good days and the days when everything feels a little heavier. I get to love you not just on the days when you're at your happiest but also on the days when you need someone to remind you that you don't have to carry everything alone.

I hope this birthday reminds you of how deeply loved you are—not only by me, but by the people whose lives you've touched just by being yourself. I hope you know how much warmth you bring into the lives around you, even when you don't realize you're doing it.

And selfishly, I hope you know how much you mean to me.

You have become my comfort, my favorite person to talk to, my little piece of peace in the middle of everything. somewhere along the way, the person I never expected became the person I find myself wanting to choose, again and again.

So on your birthday, I don't just wish you happiness. I wish you a life that feels gentle. I wish for you dreams that come true, days that don't feel too heavy, and people who will always make you feel safe, seen, and loved. I hope this new chapter gives you more reasons to smile, more moments worth remembering, and more opportunities to become the woman you've always wanted to be.

And if you'll let me, I want to be there for as much of it as you’ll have me.

Happy birthday, my love. Thank you for being the most unexpected plot twist in my life—and somehow, the one I never want to write out of the story.
